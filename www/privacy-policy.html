QuickQuote Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

QuickQuote Pro is provided by Budsproductions. This Privacy Policy explains what information QuickQuote Pro collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

Information we collect

When you create and use a QuickQuote Pro account, the service may store information you enter into the app, including:

  • Your account email address and authentication information.
  • Business profile information such as business name, phone number, email, address, website, and license information.
  • Customer information that you choose to enter, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, and notes.
  • Estimate information, including estimate numbers, descriptions, items, pricing, tax settings, expiration dates, status, and approval information.
  • Saved service and pricing information and estimate preferences.

How information is used

This information is used to provide QuickQuote Pro features, including account access, saving and synchronizing your business data, creating estimates, generating estimate PDFs, and sharing estimates with customers when you choose to do so.

Estimate sharing

When you publish an estimate for customer review, QuickQuote Pro creates a customer-accessible estimate link. Anyone who receives that link may be able to view the information included in the published estimate. Do not include information in a published estimate that you do not want to share with the recipient.

Service providers

QuickQuote Pro uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage. Data submitted through the app is processed by Supabase as necessary to provide the service.

Data retention

Your account-associated QuickQuote data is retained while your account remains active, unless you request deletion or applicable law requires otherwise.

Account and data deletion

QuickQuote Pro provides an account deletion option inside the app under Settings → Account. Users can also request account and associated data deletion through the public account deletion resource:

QuickQuote Pro Account Deletion

When an account deletion request is completed, associated QuickQuote application data and the authentication account are deleted, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons. Any required retention will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Security

We use authentication and access controls provided by our service infrastructure to protect account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

QuickQuote Pro is a business productivity application and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QuickQuote Pro's data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this address with a new revision date.
